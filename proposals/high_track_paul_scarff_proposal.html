01

The Problem

Construction equipment dealers are losing deals every week to a problem that has nothing to do with their inventory, their pricing, or their team. They are losing deals to time. A buyer submits an inquiry on Tuesday night and the dealer's first call comes Thursday morning. That buyer already bought from someone else.

  • 47-Hour Average Response Time — Industry data puts the average dealer response to a web inquiry at 47 hours. The buyer's expectation is 5 minutes. That gap is where deals go to die.
  • No Follow-Up System — If the first call goes to voicemail, most dealers mark the lead cold. There is no structured sequence. No day-two attempt. No day-five email. A warm lead becomes a ghost in less than a week.
  • Zero Post-Sale Retention — After the deal closes, contact stops. Maintenance reminders, attachment upsells, and service calls depend on whoever remembered to make them. Most of the lifetime value of that customer walks away uncaptured.
  • No Technology Built for This Market — The automotive dealer AI tools (Podium, VinSolutions, Dealer.com) were built for car lots. They do not understand equipment language, equipment timelines, or equipment buyers. No one has built this for construction. Yet.

02

The Solution — MiniMe™ Equipment Division

MiniMe™ Equipment Division is a white-label AI operating system built specifically for construction and heavy equipment dealers. It sits between the dealer's lead sources and their CRM, handling the first contact, qualification, demo booking, and post-sale retention automatically.

A buyer submits a form. Ninety seconds later, they receive a phone call from an AI that sounds like a knowledgeable equipment representative, asks the right qualification questions, and books a demo on the spot. The dealer never touches the lead until it is warm, qualified, and scheduled.

How It Works

Layer 01 — Lead Capture

Every lead source feeds into MiniMe™: web forms, Facebook Lead Ads, OEM referral portals, and walk-in registration. No lead enters the system untracked.

Layer 02 — Instant Response (under 90 seconds)

An AI voice agent calls the lead within 90 seconds of capture. An SMS arrives within 2 minutes. An email sequence starts within 5 minutes. The buyer gets a response before they can open the next tab.

Layer 03 — Qualification Engine

The AI runs a natural, conversational qualification: equipment type, timeline, budget range, financing interest, trade-in status. Every answer is logged to the CRM automatically. No manual data entry.

Layer 04 — Demo Booking

Qualified leads are transferred to the dealer's calendar. The AI handles objection handling, time zone coordination, and confirmation reminders. The dealer's sales rep shows up to a booked meeting, not a cold call.

Layer 05 — Post-Sale Retention

After close, MiniMe™ initiates automated maintenance reminders, attachment upsell sequences, and service appointment scheduling. The lifetime value of each customer goes up without any additional dealer effort.

The Competitive Gap

No platform does this specifically for construction equipment dealers. Podium handles reviews and messaging. Dealer.com manages OEM web presence. VinSolutions was built for automotive. None of them speak equipment. None of them have dealer relationships in this vertical. That is the gap this partnership occupies.
